# Final paper: VakilBuddy

`final.tex` is the combined paper (11 pages, IEEE conference format), in two parts.

- **Pages 1-5, the research** (`p1_research.tex`): "Small Models' Hit Rate on Deciding Judgments". Introduction, related work, the audited index, the citation verifier, the Supreme Court citation graph, retrieval, the benchmark of four open-weight models and its results. It is the research paper of `../ieee/main.tex`, shortened.
- **Pages 6-11, the system** (`p2_system.tex`): VakilBuddy's architecture, features, workflow and approval gate, source checks, evidence/dates/drafts, a worked Supreme Court case, limitations and ethics, conclusion, references. It does not use the unsupported parts of the earlier "LegalAI Research" draft (`../ieee/legalai_10page.tex`): the six-agent LangGraph architecture, its latency and ablation tables, and its degree-distribution figure have no data behind them in this repository.

`final_v1.tex` (with `sec_system.tex`, `sec_case.tex`, `tab_ground.tex`) is the earlier single-narrative version of the same paper; it still builds.

## Rebuild

```
# 1. the worked case (needs the case-law API on :8090 and, for the model part, Ollama with qwen2.5vl:7b)
node paper/final/case_study/run_case.mjs
node paper/final/case_study/run_grounding.mjs      # slow on a CPU; ONLY=<action> re-runs one action
node paper/final/case_study/make_case_tex.mjs      # writes numbers_case.tex and tab_case.tex, and runs the workspace test suites
node paper/final/make_figs_tex.mjs                 # writes numbers_figs.tex

# 2. the paper
cd paper/final
pdflatex final && bibtex final && pdflatex final && pdflatex final
```

The research numbers (`../ieee/numbers.tex`) are not regenerated here. To regenerate them, follow the rebuild order in `paper/experiment`.

## Before submitting

- Check the two Supreme Court references (SUVAS, SUPACE) in `refs_extra.bib`: they came from the earlier draft and have no URL.
- The paper says the workspace's citation check is simpler than the evaluated verifier and that the evaluated verifier and retriever are research code not connected to the workspace. If that changes, change Sections IX and XI.
- The acknowledgment discloses the use of an AI assistant. The authors, their order and the affiliation line are for the authors and their guide to confirm.
- Check the venue's page limit; this is 11 pages including references.
